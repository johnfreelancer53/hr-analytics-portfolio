# Employee Attrition Analysis

## 🎯 Objective

To identify key drivers of employee turnover and recommend retention strategies.

---

## 📊 Key Metrics Analyzed

* Attrition rate
* Department-wise turnover
* Tenure distribution
* Salary vs attrition correlation

---

## 📈 Insights

* High attrition in first 6 months
* Lower retention in specific departments
* Compensation misalignment impacts retention

---

## 📌 Recommendations

* Improve onboarding experience
* Conduct stay interviews
* Review compensation structures

---

* Excel
